actions but cannot guarantee the same identity or exact output across new runs.

Before-and-After: 2 earlier outputs (Mika and Italy), their exact initial prompts,
and the historical edit prompts. The edit prompts require the included base
image. Read Before-and-After/README.txt; keep canonical final asset names.

Canvas-source-prompts: available original Canvas text, including earlier variants.
These are study material, not all final approved recipes. Some require references.
Do not replace a current Recreate prompt with an earlier draft by accident.

Scene-source-prompts: the current Notion workflow recipes, with their original
asset handles and timing. They are not claimed to be recovered generation
parameters for every illustrated take. Selected takes and the final edit can
have different lengths. The three article generation plans are 18s, 19s and 4s.

Claude-requests: the saved requests from the delivered walkthrough, including
initial attempts and later corrections. Read in C-number order. They are
separate from the final, ready-to-run generation prompts and may refer to the
included project assets. Historical requests are preserved as shown on screen.

Skill/SKILL.md: updated higgsfield-timed-prompts, including blocking diagrams,
overhead views and foreground wipes. Skill/higgsfield-timed-prompts.md is the
identical raw attachment shown in C-18. The separate skill ZIP is for installation.
